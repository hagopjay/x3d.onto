# Ontology Dyson sphere (toy demo, private)

`demo.html` (X_ITE 16.5.0 from the CDN) plays `scenes/ontology-dyson.x3d`: three modules, then one assembled sphere. Spec: `DEMO-SPEC.md`. Open via the local server: `cd ontology && python3 -m http.server 8791` then `/demo/demo.html`. Rebuild: `~/Projects/x3d/x3d_mcp/.venv/bin/python demo/make_demo.py` (about 11 s), then `audit_numbers.py`.

**What you see.** Row view: the loop ring (7 stations, a packet that changes colour through the story, a report packet that flies back from x3d_mcp to the agent), the ontology shell (364 classes of X3D Ontology 4.1, the 9 new classes pulsing green, Shape's 68 parent placements as arcs coloured by XSD verdict), and three domain rings (X3D's 37 components, URDF's 24 and glTF's 25 fault operators) with ghost pillars for the model evaluation. **Assemble** (button or the sign in the scene) flies the camera in over 16 s, shrinks the loop and the rings into the shell, flares the star and fades the captions; **Disassemble** reverses it; **Orbit** is a slow loop camera.

**Built through x3d_mcp**: 6,338 tool calls in 11 s (median 1.7 ms), 268 KB. `validate_x3d` valid against XSD 4.1; `validate_semantic` 0 errors, 0 warnings, 381 infos (unused DEFs). Routes: 2 lap and assemble clocks with interpolators, the camera flights, the pulse of the new classes.
**Number audit** (`audit_numbers.py`, exit 0): the 12 standalone numbers in the scene text are all derivable from a file in this folder or from `sources.json` (E1 first try 27.3% to 56.7%, about 100% after the loop, 6 of 6 provenance, from DOC.md, graded C).

**Honest limits.** Display choices: layout, colours, motion; timings in captions are quoted, not played in real time. The evaluation pillars are ghosted until the model runs finish; when `eval-plan/runs/{x3d,urdf,gltf}-gemini-3.8-flash.jsonl` hold at least 30 first tries and a finished report-loop arm, re-running `make_demo.py` fills them from the data and `audit_numbers.py` checks the percentages. The simulated sc: layer and everything about the Shroud are not in this demo. Not yet checked: X3DOM or a second runtime, performance on weaker GPUs, a video capture. Private until 2027-01-01.

## Film, curator tour, tooltips, sound (added 2026-10-21)
- **Film** (button or F): a 75 s autoplay with letterbox bars, chapter cards, a camera path baked into the scene (`FilmClock`, 151-key interpolators) and the assemble sequence triggered inside the scene at 44 s (`Delay44` -> `TimeTrigger` -> `AssembleFilm`). Skip with Esc. After any assemble the page reloads for a clean replay.
- **Curator tour** (T): 11 stops, Back/Next, arrow keys or 1 to 9, a halo that marks the thing under discussion, a "what to look for" line, the source file and the grade of each stop; "Free roam" leaves the path at any time. Text is generated by `build_tour.py` from the data files; every number must be a recorded fact with a source (it exits 1 otherwise; `out/demo_tour_facts.json`).
- **Tooltips**: 463 touch sensors added through x3d_mcp (364 classes, 7 stations, 86 ring beads, 6 pillars). Hover for definition, XSD verdict counts, fault description and who catches it, or the measured number with its grade; click pins. Toolbar buttons have tips too.
- **Sound**: a synthesised drone, chapter arpeggios, an assemble whoosh and a shockwave boom, all WebAudio with no audio files; M toggles, the choice is remembered. Starts on the first click (browser autoplay rule). Not heard by me: only the code path ran.
- Rebuild: `make_demo.py` (also runs `build_tour.py`), then `audit_numbers.py`. Scene: 7,928 tool calls in 15 s, 313 KB, valid XSD 4.1, 0 semantic errors.
