Gobbler 4, step 1: live hover on the 3 Deep scene

The scene is the Gobbler 3 Deep file, byte for byte. This page adds a layer above it: point at a star and it snaps to the nearest one; rest on it and more of the knowledge graph opens, up to a card docked in the emptiest corner. Nothing is sent anywhere; every word is read from the graph already in the page. Each card says how we know a thing and how sure we are.
